## 07_suffix_near_misses: Suffix near-misses (GP200 vs GP200S)

- **Check:** Within one GUDID manufacturer, finds pairs of usable model keys where the longer key is the shorter key plus 1-3 trailing characters (shorter key at least 3 characters and containing a digit).
